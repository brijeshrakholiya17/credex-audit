# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Objective
Our primary goal is to use the **AI Spend Audit** as a lead generation engine for Credex.
- **Target:** 5,000 Monthly Active Users (MAU) evaluating their spend.
- **Conversion Goal:** 100 Qualified Leads per month (users with >$500/mo in potential savings who submit their email).

## 2. Target Audience
- **Startup Founders (Seed - Series A):** Cost-conscious, paying for tools out of pocket or via early funding, managing subscriptions manually via corporate cards.
- **Engineering Managers / CTOs:** Managing teams of 10-50 developers, dealing with fragmented tool requests (e.g., half the team wants Cursor, half wants Copilot).

## 3. Acquisition Channels & Real Numbers

### Budget: $2,000 / month

**A. Paid Acquisition (LinkedIn & Twitter Ads)**
- **Allocation:** $1,000/mo
- **Targeting:** "CTO", "VP Engineering", "Founder" at companies with 10-200 employees.
- **Estimated CPC:** $3.50
- **Target CPA (Cost Per Lead):** $15.00
- **Expected Output:** ~66 highly qualified leads.

**B. ProductHunt Launch**
- **Goal:** Top 3 Product of the Day.
- **Estimated Traffic:** 2,500 unique visitors in 48 hours.
- **Conversion Rate:** 8% to audit completion -> 200 audits.
- **Expected Output:** ~30 qualified leads (assuming 15% high-savings rate).

**C. Organic Social & Viral Loop**
- **Strategy:** Post tear-downs of "The average Series A company wastes $4,000/yr on duplicate AI tools" on Twitter/X.
- **Viral Mechanism:** The built-in "Share results" button generates a unique link.
- **K-Factor Assumption:** 0.15 (For every 100 users, 15 new users are acquired via shared links).

## 4. Activation & Retention
Because this is a one-off audit tool, retention is not the primary metric—activation (email capture) is.
- **Hook:** "Find out if you're overpaying in 2 minutes."
- **Aha Moment:** Seeing the big red "$X/mo Potential Savings" number.
- **Next Step:** Nurturing captured emails via a 3-part drip campaign offering a deeper, manual Credex consultation.
