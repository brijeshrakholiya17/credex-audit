# Product Metrics

## North Star Metric
**Qualified Leads Per Week**
*Definition:* The total number of users who successfully complete the audit and provide their email address.

## Key Inputs (Levers)

1. **Audit Completion Rate**
   - *Description:* The percentage of users who start the audit (click "Audit my AI spend" on the landing page) and successfully complete the 2-step form to view their results.
   - *Optimization:* Improve the form UX, reduce friction, clarify tool selection.

2. **Email Capture Rate**
   - *Description:* The percentage of users who view their audit results and submit their email address via the capture modal.
   - *Optimization:* A/B test the CTA copy (e.g., "Notify me of future savings"), offer additional value (like an in-depth PDF report).

3. **High-Savings Rate**
   - *Description:* The percentage of completed audits that yield potential monthly savings of >$500, triggering the "Book a Credex Consultation" CTA.
   - *Optimization:* Drive traffic from larger teams or enterprise segments who are more likely to have bloated, overlapping AI stacks.
